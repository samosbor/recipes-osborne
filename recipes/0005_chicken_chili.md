# Chicken Chili
0005

![Chicken Chili](../images/0005_chicken_chili.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast
- 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 4 cans Great Northern beans
- 1 (14 1/2 oz) can chicken broth
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 cup whip cream or evap milk
- 1 medium onion
- 1 tbsp veg oil
- 1 can green chilis
- 1 tsp cummin
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 cup sour cream

## Instructions

1. Sauté chicken, onion, garlic powder in oil until chicken not pink.
2. Then add beans, broth, chilis, seasoning.
3. Simmer 30 minutes.
4. Stir in creams.
