# Spicy Beef Noodle Stir Fry
0016

![20 Minute Spicy Beef Noodle Stir Fry](../images/0016_spicy_beef_noodle_stir_fry.webp)

## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 tbsp chili oil
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp vegetarian oyster sauce
- 1 1/2 tsp sugar
- 2 packs QQ noodles, or another sturdy noodle such as frozen udon

### Optional Garnish

- Sesame seeds
- Green onion, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Brown the Beef:** Saute the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes, until browned.
2. **Cook the Onion:** Add the chopped onion and saute for 2-3 minutes, until softened.
3. **Season:** Stir in the chili oil, soy sauce, vegetarian oyster sauce, and sugar. Saute for 1 minute.
4. **Add Noodles:** Add the cooked noodles and saute over high heat for 2-3 minutes, tossing until evenly coated and heated through.
5. **Serve:** Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped green onion, if using.
