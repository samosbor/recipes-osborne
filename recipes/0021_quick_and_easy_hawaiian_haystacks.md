# Quick and Easy Hawaiian Haystacks
0021

![Quick and Easy Hawaiian Haystacks](../images/0021_quick_and_easy_hawaiian_haystacks.jpg)

## Ingredients

### Chicken Gravy and Rice

- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of chicken soup with herbs
- 8 oz sour cream
- 1/2 cup milk
- 3 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 6 cups prepared hot rice

### Toppings

- Shredded cheese
- Pineapple tidbits
- Sliced black olives
- Diced tomatoes
- Diced green onions
- Frozen green peas, thawed
- Crunchy chow mein noodles
- Shredded carrots

## Instructions

1. **Make the Chicken Gravy:** In a large skillet, whisk together the cream of chicken soup, sour cream, and milk over medium heat until smooth and heated through.
2. **Add the Chicken:** Stir in the shredded chicken and cook until warmed through.
3. **Assemble the Haystacks:** Spoon the chicken gravy over hot rice and add any combination of the suggested toppings.

## Notes

- Serves 8.
- If cream of chicken soup with herbs is unavailable, use regular cream of chicken soup and add 1 tsp garlic powder and 1 tsp Italian seasoning.
- Recipe adapted from [I Wash You Dry](https://iwashyoudry.com/quick-easy-hawaiian-haystacks/).
