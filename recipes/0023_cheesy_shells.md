# Cheesy Shells
0023

![Cheesy Shells](../images/0023_cheesy_shells.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces medium shell pasta
- 1 pound ground beef
- 15 ounces beef broth
- 8 ounces heavy cream
- 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 24 ounces marinara sauce
- 2 tablespoons minced garlic
- 1 white onion, diced
- 1 teaspoon parsley
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 2 teaspoons oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Brown the beef:** Cook the ground beef with the onion, garlic, parsley, Italian seasoning, oregano, salt, and pepper.
2. **Add the pasta:** Stir in the marinara sauce, beef broth, and dry pasta. Bring to a boil.
3. **Simmer:** Cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook for 25 minutes, stirring often.
4. **Make it creamy:** When the pasta is tender, stir in the heavy cream and cheddar.
5. **Rest:** Turn off the heat, cover, and let stand for 10 minutes before serving.
