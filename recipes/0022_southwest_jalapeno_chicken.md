# Southwest Jalapeno Chicken
0022

![Southwest Jalapeno Chicken](../images/0022_southwest_jalapeno_chicken.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 3 to 4 large chicken breasts, cut into chunks
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon ancho chile powder
- 3 cups frozen corn kernels
- 1 to 2 jalapenos, diced, or 7 ounces diced green chiles
- 3 teaspoons minced garlic
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 3 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 cup shredded cheese, plus 1/2 cup for topping
- 2 tablespoons diced cilantro, optional
- Diced bacon crumbles, optional

## Instructions

1. **Season the chicken:** Combine the chili powder, oregano, salt, cumin, pepper, onion powder, and ancho chile powder. Coat the chicken pieces in the seasoning mixture.
2. **Cook the chicken:** Heat the oil in a skillet and fry the chicken until cooked through. Remove it from the skillet.
3. **Finish the skillet:** Add the corn, jalapenos or green chiles, garlic, bell pepper, lime juice, 1 cup cheese, and cilantro or bacon if using. Stir, then return the chicken to the skillet.
4. **Melt the topping:** Sprinkle with the remaining 1/2 cup cheese. Cover until melted, then serve.
