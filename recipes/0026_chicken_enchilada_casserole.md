# Chicken Enchilada Casserole
0026

![Chicken Enchilada Casserole](../images/0026_chicken_enchilada_casserole.png)

## Ingredients

- 4 cups cooked or rotisserie chicken breast
- 20 ounces red enchilada sauce
- 4 ounces diced green chiles
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 16 ounces freshly shredded Colby Jack cheese
- 14 ounces fire-roasted canned corn
- 8 ounces plain cream cheese, softened
- 12 white or yellow corn tortillas

## Instructions

1. **Heat the oven:** Preheat the oven to 375 F.
2. **Make the filling:** Mix the chicken, taco seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, chili powder, corn, green chiles, and cream cheese until well combined.
3. **Start the casserole:** Spread 2 tablespoons enchilada sauce in an even thin layer in a 13-by-9-inch casserole dish. Arrange 6 tortillas over the sauce.
4. **Layer:** Top with half the chicken mixture, a drizzle of enchilada sauce, and a layer of Colby Jack. Repeat for a second layer.
5. **Bake and rest:** Bake uncovered for 30 to 35 minutes. Let cool for 10 to 15 minutes before serving.
